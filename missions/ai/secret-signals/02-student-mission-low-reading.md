# Secret Signals 🚀 (Short Version)

**Teach the computer 2 hand signals.**

| ✋ | ✊ | ⬜ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Open hand** | **Closed fist** | **No signal** |

**Work with a buddy.** One shows the hand. One clicks.

## ⭐ Rules

- 📷 Only **hands** in the camera. **No faces.**
- 🚫 Do **not** click **Save** or **Export**.
- 🙂 It's OK if the computer makes mistakes!

## 🛠️ Build it

1. Go to **teachablemachine.withgoogle.com**.
2. Click **Get Started**.
3. Click **Image Project**. Then click **Standard image model**.
4. Name the boxes: **Open hand**, **Closed fist**.
5. Click **Add a class**. Name it **No signal**.

## 📷 Teach it

6. Click **Webcam**.
7. Show ✋. **Hold down Hold to Record.** Count "1." Let go. Do it 2 times.
8. Do the same for ✊.
9. For **No signal**, show the wall. No hand!

## 🧠 Train it

10. Click **Train Model**. Wait.

## 🔍 Test it

11. **Guess first:** what will it say if you turn your hand sideways?
12. Show ✋, then ✊, then nothing.
13. Look at the bars. The longest bar is the computer's guess.
14. ✅ or ❌? Mark your table.

## 🔄 Change one thing

15. Turn your hand sideways, **or** move far away, **or** use a new wall.
16. Test again. Did it mess up?

## 💪 Make it better

17. Teach it more. Show your hand in **new** ways. Show it sideways and far away.
18. Click **Train Model** again.
19. Do the **same** tests. Is it better?

## 🚀 Challenge

**Fix a spot where it got confused.** Draw or write:

- ❌ What went wrong?
- ➕ What did you add?
- ✅ What happened next?
- 🤔 What can still trick it?

## 💬 Talk about it

- The computer learned from your **examples**.
- New hands it never saw are **tests**.
- **Different** examples help more than **the same** example over and over.

**All done?** Close the tab. 👋

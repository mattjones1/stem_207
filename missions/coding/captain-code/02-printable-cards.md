# Captain Code: printable cards

Print one warm-up card per group, and one Plot your course sheet and one exit ticket per student.

---

## Human-boat warm-up

**One person is the boat. Everyone else is the programmer.**

1. The boat stands on the dock square and faces the "sea".
2. The programmers write the route **before** the boat moves, using only:
   - **Forward** (one step)
   - **Turn left** (a quarter turn, no step)
   - **Turn right** (a quarter turn, no step)
3. Read the program out loud. The boat does **exactly** what it hears, even if it's wrong.
4. Did the boat reach the mooring? If not, find the **first** wrong step and fix only that one.

**Remember:** left and right are the **boat's** left and right, not yours.

*Instructor: tape a dock and a mooring 3 or 4 squares apart with one turn between them. Then add a "trap" the boat must stop on and say "Haul!"*

**Key:** **S** your boat · **G** your mooring · **#** granite ledge · **\*** trap buoy

1. Draw your ledges, traps, and mooring.
2. Draw the route with arrows.
3. Circle any part of the route that **repeats**. That's where a loop or your own block goes.

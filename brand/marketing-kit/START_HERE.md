## Before using
The first three designs can introduce the brand with an accurate caption and verified link. The flyer and partner sheet require every bracketed detail to be completed before sharing. The technology imagery is illustrative, not evidence of actual facilities, equipment, software interfaces or program availability. Advertise coding, AI, VR or AR sessions only when their curriculum, equipment and delivery arrangements are confirmed. The generated code and AI graphic are decorative, not instructional examples.

## Editing and exporting
Open SVG files in Inkscape or another compatible vector editor. Headline and body text are editable; artwork is embedded as a bitmap, and the original logo remains vector. Typeface: DejaVu Sans, regular and bold. Install that font if your editor substitutes it. Keep text inside its allotted area; shorten long workshop names or use two lines. Update the SVG, then export new PNG/PDF copies. PDFs have outlined text for consistent printing and are not fillable forms.

Flyer and partner PDFs are 8.5 x 11 inches, intended for ordinary office printing. Print at actual size; edge-to-edge artwork may require a borderless printer. These are not commercial bleed-ready files. PNG print-template copies are for previews; use PDFs for printing. Concept artwork is raster; don't enlarge it for large-format signage.

## Color and hierarchy
- Indigo #302568: logo, headings, mission, readable body copy.
- Lime #8CC832: energy, underlines and calls to action. Dark text on lime.
- Warm paper #FFF8E9: welcoming base.
- Sky #91D9FA: supporting accent from the approved vision direction.

Logo first, invitation second, one clear next step, mission always. Preserve logo proportions. Do not use generated renderings of the logo in final collateral.
